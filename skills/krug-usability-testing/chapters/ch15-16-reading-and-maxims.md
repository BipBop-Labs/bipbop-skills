# Chapters 15–16: Overachievers only / Happy trails to you

## Core Idea
Ch 15 lists where to go deeper once you're testing regularly. Ch 16 collects the six maxims, the whole method in six lines. Everything is a recommendation, so experiment and do what works.

## The Maxims (Ch 16, verbatim)
1. **A morning a month, that's all we ask.** (ch03)
2. **Start earlier than you think makes sense.** (ch04)
3. **Recruit loosely and grade on a curve.** (ch05)
4. **Make it a spectator sport.** (ch09)
5. **Focus ruthlessly on a small number of the most important problems.** (ch10)
6. **When fixing problems, always do the least you can do.** (ch11)

Plus **Krug's First Law of Usability Testing**: *Testing with one user is 100% better than testing with none.* (ch05)

## Recommended Reading (Ch 15)
**Usability testing in general** (read one after you've started testing):
- *Handbook of Usability Testing*, 2nd ed., by Jeffrey Rubin and Dana Chisnell.
- *A Practical Guide to Usability Testing*, rev. ed., by Joseph Dumas and Janice (Ginny) Redish.
- *Usability Testing Essentials*, by Carol Barnum (covers accessibility and international testing).

**Specific topics**:
- *Paper Prototyping*, by Carolyn Snyder: everything on paper prototypes, and a good general testing book.
- *Moderating Usability Tests*, by Joseph Dumas and Beth Loring: facilitation in depth.
- *Measuring the User Experience*, by Thomas Tullis and William Albert: required reading if you must do **quantitative** or benchmark testing.
- Recruiting reports from UIE (*Recruiting Without Fear*) and Nielsen Norman Group (*233 Tips and Tricks for Recruiting Users*).

**Fixing things**:
- *Letting Go of the Words*, by Ginny Redish: fixing problems caused by writing.
- *Forms that Work*, by Caroline Jarrett and Gerry Gaffney: fixing forms.

## Key Takeaways
1. If you remember nothing else, remember the six maxims.
2. Go deeper only after you've started. Doing beats studying.
3. Reach for *Measuring the User Experience* when someone needs proof, because DIY testing isn't designed to provide it.
